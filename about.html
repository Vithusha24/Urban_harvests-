Our Story

Urban Harvest began in 2024 as a small community initiative on a rooftop in Brooklyn. Frustrated by the lack of access to fresh produce and the overwhelming amount of plastic waste in our city, a group of neighbors decided to take matters into their own hands.

Today, we are a growing movement helping thousands of city dwellers start their own gardens, reduce waste, and live more consciously. We believe that small, mindful choices can create a big difference for the planet.
